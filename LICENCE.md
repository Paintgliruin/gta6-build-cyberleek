## 🧩 About the Leak

In August 2026, an individual or group operating as **Cyberleak** released extensive gameplay footage from an **early Alpha build of Grand Theft Auto VI**. The leaker proved direct access to a playable build by spelling "LEEK" with bullet holes in-game.

**Key Details:**
- **Build Era:** 2022 – early 2023 
- **Leak Start:** August 16, 2026 
- **Content:** Over 2 hours of gameplay 
- **Map:** Full Vice City region 
- **Confirmed:** Playable build access 
- **Protagonists:** Jason appears in all clips; Lucia not yet shown 
- **Status:** 15+ gigabytes of footage, screenshots, and documentation
